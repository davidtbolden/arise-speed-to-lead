The conversion gap

Your ads may be working. The follow-through may not be.

Dental groups invest in demand generation, but a lead can still stall when it arrives during a busy check-in, after hours, or at a location with a callback backlog.

01Response performance varies by location and shift.
02Paid leads enter voicemail or delayed callback queues.
03Campaign reporting stops at calls and form fills.
04Marketing and operations see different versions of the outcome.
How the joint solution works

One controlled journey from lead source to patient outcome.

Start with a single, measurable workflow. Expand only after the technical, operational, and patient-experience fit is proven.

Implementation guardrails

Useful claims have boundaries.

Attribution

Campaign-to-outcome reporting applies where integrations, permissions, and the client’s data access support it.

Booking

Direct booking is offered only where the selected PMS and operational rules support the required workflow.

Compliance

The solution uses implementation-specific safeguards; it does not make a client compliant on its own.

Performance

No outcome is guaranteed. Results are measured against an agreed, customer-approved baseline.
